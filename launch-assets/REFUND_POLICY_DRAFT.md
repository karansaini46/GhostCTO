# Refund Policy Draft

This is an operational draft for the GhostCTO launch offer. Review before publishing.

## Suggested Policy

GhostCTO offers a 7-day refund window for the $99 lifetime access purchase.

If GhostCTO is not the right fit, contact support within 7 days of purchase with the email address used at checkout and the reason for the request.

## Eligible Refund Reasons

Refunds may be approved when:

- You purchased by mistake.
- You made a duplicate purchase.
- You cannot access the product after reasonable support attempts.
- You tried the product and it does not fit your current stage or use case.

## Refund Boundaries

Refunds may be declined when:

- The request is made more than 7 days after purchase.
- The account shows substantial use across multiple project documents, exports, or reviews.
- The purchase has already been disputed, charged back, or refunded through the payment provider.
- The request is connected to abuse, license sharing, or activity that violates product terms.

## What Happens After A Refund

After a refund is approved:

- Lifetime access is removed from the account.
- The license key can no longer be used.
- Access returns to the free plan limits, where available.
- Any payment provider processing time is controlled by the provider and the customer's bank.

## Support Request Template

Subject: Refund request

Please include:

- Purchaser email
- Approximate purchase date
- License key last four characters, if available
- Reason for the request
- Any access issue or billing issue details

## Customer-Facing Version

GhostCTO offers a 7-day refund window for the $99 lifetime access purchase.

If it is not the right fit, email support within 7 days of purchase with the email address used at checkout and a short note about the issue.

Approved refunds remove lifetime access and return the account to the free plan limits, where available. Refund timing depends on the payment provider and the customer's bank.

Refunds may be declined after the 7-day window, after substantial product use, after a payment dispute or chargeback, or in cases of license abuse.
