# FAQ

## What is GhostCTO?

GhostCTO is a technical planning workspace for non-technical founders. It helps you turn an idea into clearer scope, stack guidance, hiring materials, quote review notes, and developer-ready documents.

## Who is GhostCTO for?

GhostCTO is for founders who are close to spending money on software but do not yet have a technical co-founder, CTO, or trusted engineering advisor.

It is especially useful if you are preparing to hire a freelancer, agency, first developer, or technical consultant.

## What problem does it solve?

It helps founders reduce avoidable confusion before the first build starts.

Common problems include vague feature lists, unclear milestones, stack decisions you cannot evaluate, developer quotes that are hard to compare, and interviews where every answer sounds convincing.

## What do I get for $99?

The launch offer is $99 one-time for lifetime access.

You get a founder workspace for project scoping, stack advice, technical specs, hiring packets, developer vetting, quote analysis, targeted code review, and PDF exports.

## Does GhostCTO replace a CTO?

No. GhostCTO gives founders stronger technical context and clearer documents, but complex products still benefit from experienced technical leadership.

## Does GhostCTO guarantee I will hire the right developer?

No. GhostCTO helps you ask better questions, compare candidates more clearly, and spot common risk signals. It cannot guarantee hiring outcomes.

## Does GhostCTO tell me exactly what my app should cost?

No. Custom software costs depend on scope, team quality, location, complexity, integrations, speed, and risk.

GhostCTO helps you understand likely cost drivers, challenge unclear estimates, and identify assumptions that should be clarified before you sign.

## Can GhostCTO review developer quotes?

Yes. You can use GhostCTO to review proposal assumptions, vague milestones, missing deliverables, timeline risks, ownership questions, and red flags.

It does not replace legal review of contracts or statements of work.

## Can GhostCTO review code?

GhostCTO can help with targeted review of code snippets, repositories, architecture notes, and implementation choices.

It can flag maintainability concerns, missing context, risky assumptions, and obvious security hygiene issues. It is not a complete security audit, penetration test, or compliance review.

## Do I need technical experience?

No. GhostCTO is written for founders who need plain-language technical decisions, not engineering jargon.

## What kinds of products can I use it for?

GhostCTO is best suited for early-stage SaaS products, marketplaces, mobile or web apps, internal tools, workflow automation, and founder-led software projects.

It is less suited for deep hardware, regulated medical systems, defense systems, or highly specialized infrastructure without expert review.

## Can I use GhostCTO before I have a full idea?

Yes, but the output will be more useful when you can describe the customer, problem, must-have features, business model, budget range, and launch timeline.

## Can I use it after I already hired a developer?

Yes. Founders can use it to clarify scope changes, review new estimates, prepare technical specs, and keep vendor conversations focused.

## Is GhostCTO a developer marketplace?

No. GhostCTO does not match you with developers. It helps you prepare before you hire, brief, or negotiate with them.

## How should I use the documents?

Use them as working materials for developer calls, quote comparison, internal planning, advisor review, and scope discussions.

The documents are decision support. You should still apply judgment and involve qualified experts for legal, compliance, security, or highly specialized technical decisions.

## What should I prepare before using GhostCTO?

Prepare a short description of your idea, target customer, must-have features, budget range, launch timeline, current assets, and biggest concerns.

If you already have a proposal, quote, code sample, or candidate profile, bring that context into the review workflow.

## Why should I use GhostCTO before hiring?

Because unclear scope and weak technical context make it harder to judge developers, compare quotes, and control the build.

GhostCTO helps you start with a clearer plan before the expensive work begins.
