### First Comment

Hi Product Hunt,

GhostCTO was built for the moment when a founder is ready to spend money on software but does not yet have a technical person they fully trust.

That is a risky moment. The founder knows the customer and business problem, but every developer call starts creating technical decisions: scope, stack, milestones, pricing, timeline, hosting, payments, auth, handoff, and support.

GhostCTO helps turn that uncertainty into founder-ready planning materials:

- MVP scope brief with explicit exclusions
- Stack recommendation tied to budget and timeline
- Technical specs and acceptance criteria
- Hiring questions and evaluation rubrics
- Quote review notes for assumptions, cost drivers, and red flags
- PDF exports for vendor calls and advisor review

The promise is intentionally sharp:

Before you hire a developer, spend $99 and know what to build, what stack to use, what to ask, what it should cost, and what red flags to avoid.

GhostCTO does not guarantee hiring outcomes, replace legal review, or perform complete security audits. It gives founders better technical context before expensive decisions.

Would value feedback from founders who have hired developers without a technical co-founder.

### Problem

Most founders do not overspend because they cannot write code.

They overspend because the scope is vague, the stack recommendation is hard to challenge, the quote has hidden assumptions, and the hiring interview does not reveal enough signal.

GhostCTO gives founders a practical technical planning workspace before the build starts.

- Scope briefs developers can estimate
- Stack recommendations with plain-language tradeoffs
- Technical specs with acceptance criteria
- Hiring packets with screening questions and evaluation rubrics

### AppSumo FAQ

#### Is GhostCTO for technical founders?

It can be useful for technical founders, but the product is written for non-technical founders who need plain-language technical decisions.

#### Will GhostCTO build my product?

No. GhostCTO helps you plan, brief, evaluate, and review before and during a build. Developers still implement the product.

#### Can I use it with my existing developer?

Yes. Use it to clarify scope changes, review proposals, prepare specs, and make technical conversations more concrete.

#### Does it tell me exactly what my app will cost?

No. It helps identify cost drivers, risk areas, and quote assumptions so you can ask better follow-up questions.

#### Does it replace a CTO?

No. It gives founders stronger technical context, but complex products still benefit from experienced technical leadership.
